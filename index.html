Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

InterviewJS is designed as a client-side practice application. It does not require an account and does not ask for your name, password, or payment information.

What stays in your browser

Your interview results, selected theme and small application preferences are stored in your browser's LocalStorage. This data remains on your device unless you clear it through the application or your browser settings.

No hidden tracking

This demo does not intentionally send interview answers or scores to a server. There are no third-party analytics calls in the application.

Contact

If you contact the project owner using the Contact page, your email client or the configured contact service may process the information you choose to send.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application changes. The latest update date is shown at the top of this page.
